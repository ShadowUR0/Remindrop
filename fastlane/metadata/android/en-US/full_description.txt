Remindrop turns anything you can share on Android into a reminder that comes back when you choose.

See a post, video, article, product, message, or link you want to revisit? Open Android's Share menu, choose Remindrop, pick a time, and continue what you were doing. Remindrop stores the reminder locally and shows a local notification later.

Features:
• Share text and links from other Android apps
• Quick presets for one hour, this evening, tomorrow, and the weekend
• Custom date and time
• Now, Later, and Done sections
• Open the original link from the reminder
• Mark reminders as done or snooze them
• Restore pending reminders after a reboot
• Material 3 interface with dynamic colors on Android 12+
• Arabic and English

Privacy:
• No account
• No ads
• No analytics or trackers
• No cloud backend
• No Internet permission
• Reminder data stays on the device

Remindrop does not run a permanent background service or polling loop. It uses Android's alarm system only when a reminder is scheduled.
